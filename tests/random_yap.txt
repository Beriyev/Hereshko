Random Yap Notes

The city pigeons appear to have established an informal transportation network.
Nobody is sure who started it, but every morning they gather on the same rooftop,
stare at the horizon for several minutes, and then disperse with remarkable confidence.

A local coffee shop accidentally created a loyalty program by forgetting to charge
one customer for a muffin. The customer felt obligated to return, and after several
months everyone involved had forgotten why the tradition started.

Meanwhile, a software developer spent three hours automating a task that only took
thirty seconds to perform manually. The automation worked perfectly and was immediately
celebrated as a major productivity improvement.

Scientists continue to investigate whether socks disappear in washing machines or
simply move on to more ambitious opportunities elsewhere.
